# GradleMC Performance Guard

The Guard has NORMAL, PRESSURED, and RECOVERING states. Three sustained observations can enter pressure after a ten-second dwell; six recovery observations are required to relax. It only marks optional GradleMC work for deferral. It never changes Minecraft, video, simulation, JVM, or third-party settings.

Modes are persisted as LOW_IMPACT, BALANCED (default), or DETAILED. DETAILED is diagnostic detail, not a game-performance claim. Commands: `/gradlemc performance`, `guard`, `explain`, `mode`, and `selftest`.
